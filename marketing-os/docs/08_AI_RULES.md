# 08 — AI Rules

## Principles

1. **Templates first, AI second** — reduce cost and improve consistency.
2. AI outputs are drafts until user/system approval rules say otherwise.
3. Log every billable AI call: workspace, feature, tokens/cost estimate, model.
4. Per-workspace budget caps (Sprint 14+); fail soft when exceeded.
5. Never claim AI will guarantee Google rankings.
6. Final social images go through the **poster template / resize engine**, not raw AI canvas sizes.
7. Keep AI provider swappable (interface + driver).

## Allowed V1 AI uses

- Captions / hashtags
- Meta title/description suggestions
- Blog outline / draft assist
- SEO task suggestions
- Optional background image generation

## Disallowed without explicit sprint

- Fully autonomous spend with no caps
- Training on customer private data for global models
- Client-specific prompt hardcoding
